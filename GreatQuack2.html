Entry 2: The Orgins of the duckfather.

The duckfather is a legendary figure in duckhood. He started it all. He it was, who created the first quack; he used it to invent silliness and therefore ducks. From these first ducks was born the duckfather. He then became the father of all ducks, and constantly keeps vigilant watch over his duckchildren. He keeps ducks secret from the outside world. You may even think that you know about the secret existence of ducks; but on deeper reflection, you will realize that you do not.

No one knows where the duckfather came from. Some say he was born from a quack. Others say he was born from an egg. But no one really knows.

Back then, ducks were just a myth. The duckfather was a duck, and therefore a myth. The duckfather was tired of being a myth. If he was just a myth, he wouldn't be real! The reason he made ducks real, and not a myth, was so that he could be a real myth and not just a duck, or a myth, but a real mythical duck that was real. Once he was real, it was time to make ducks; for real this time!

The various government agencies didn't really want ducks to be real. If ducks were real, then the duckfather would be real too! If the duckfather was real, and not just a myth, he'd invent silliness, but this time for real for real this time fr. The various government agencies wanted to be taken seriously, because they were very serious all the time. Part of the reason they were very serious all of the time was because that was really the only option; silliness hadn't been invented yet, because the duckfather was just a myth and wasn't really very real and didn't really invent silliness for real, this time. But one day, that all changed.

The duckfather quacked really, really, loudly; and also it was really silly. All those reallys made silly ducks real and really silly. They were so real that the ducks stopped being a secret from the world. Two of these ducks gave birth to an egg, and two of these eggs gave birth do a duck called the duckfather. He redacted the secret of the ducks so they were a secret again, but it was too late; they were real, and the various government organizations knew.

It's tough being the duckfather. He was quickly made the father of the ducks, on account of being the duckfather and all, and he has to constantly protect their silliness and their secret from the secret serious government organizations, who want to expose ducks to the whole world.

The various government organizations want to get their hands on all the ducks. If they do, they'll turn ducks into commonplace woodland creatures. But the duckfather will never let that happen! It does actually sometimes happen, but don't tell him that; instead tell him he's doing a great job. You never know, though, because a lot of ducks are probably just pretending. But a lot of them are secretly very intelligent and undercover and stuff. Some of them are walking among you and I. You might even be a duck, who knows! The smartest way for a duck to keep his duckness a secret is if he doesn't even know it himself, and ducks are very smart so that's probably the case in your case, in any case.

If you'd like to help the duckfather, do your part. If you think you see a duck, don't blow his cover! Keep it a secret. If you don't acknowledge him, he won't acknowledge you. Ducks aren't very good at keeping secrets, but don't tell them that. They also like snacks, so that's also a good way to help.
